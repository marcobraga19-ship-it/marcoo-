Our task was to transform a space below a long disused canopy, choosing one of its spans as the starting point for the design, that had to be based on wood. With a volume of up to 100 cubic meters available, the goal was to create a multifunctional and welcoming space for two members of the research team plus a guest, which would respond to practical needs, but which would also be an expression of a concept of flexibility and adaptability."

The transformation of a disused space under a canopy was an opportunity to respect and recover the historical character of the site, while maintaining a dialogue with contemporary needs."

This interplay reflects a space that adapts to the evolving needs of the research group, fostering creativity, collaboration, and rest within a limited area."
