Rather than dividing domestic space into conventional rooms, the project arranges activities according to layers of importance, intimacy, and frequency of use. The house becomes a personal interior landscape, where living is understood as a sequence of precise actions: opening, closing, hiding, revealing, resting, studying, caring for the body, and cultivating oneself."

Inspired by Ugo La Pietra’s research on concealment, the dwelling does not display all its functions at once. Each activity is hidden within the thickness of the architecture and revealed only when activated. Concealment becomes a way to give value to each gesture."
